##### Statement on the use of AI.

Cursor and various AIs, including Anthropic’s Claude (Opus and Fable), OpenAI’s ChatGPT (5.6 Sol), and Grok 4.5, were used in preparing this paper. The AI wrote the programs (in Python) that, when run, generated the figures; the name of the program is listed in the caption of each figure created this way, and the programs themselves are available in the Zest repository at <https://github.com/paul-stahura/zest>. AI was also used to write the LaTeX source of the paper from the author’s text and instructions. AI was also used to generate some of the code for the Zest webapp (which was based on the human-created Zest Unity app) and it was used to generate the Lean code from the author’s proofs; that code was checked both by the AI and by the author at <https://live.lean-lang.org/>. An AI also assisted in creating the literature review and the glossary. Throughout, the AI was prompted in detail by the author to complete, fill in, or explain proofs, and its output was checked, edited, and verified by the author.
